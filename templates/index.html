Why trust these numbers?

Models are evaluated on the last 20% of the timeline, in chronological order, with features built only from past information. No shuffle, no leakage, no invented weather variables. The honest result: this real dataset has near-zero day-to-day autocorrelation, so no model can beat the mean by much — the numbers above are exactly what the models achieved.
